# Inkling

Clarity on your own life, through divination. Inkling reads tarot (Celtic Cross), Lenormand (3x3 grid), and the I Ching (one hexagram). It refines your question, asks you to confirm it, then casts with a small bundled script that shuffles the cards or tosses the coins using a true random source. The answer comes first, with the full cast below it for checking and learning.

## What it does

The script only shuffles cards and tosses coins, then prints the result. It uses Python's built-in `random` and `sys` modules, makes no network calls, reads no files, and saves nothing. Inkling reads only for the person asking, about their own life, and it points anyone who mentions self-harm or danger to emergency services or a crisis line.

## Example prompts

- "Can you do a tarot reading on my creative work?"
- "Cast the I Ching for me: should I start writing every morning?"
- "Use Lenormand: what's going on with my job search?"
- "I think I'm stuck because I'm scared of being judged. Can you read on that?"
